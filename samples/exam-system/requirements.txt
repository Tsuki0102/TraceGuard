================================================================================
                    在线考试系统 - 需求规格说明书
                    Online Examination System - Requirements Specification
================================================================================

版本: 1.0
日期: 2026-08-20
状态: Draft

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REQ-001 | 创建考试 | Priority: HIGH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
系统应支持创建考试，考试信息包括：考试标题(title)、考试时长(duration，单位分钟)、
及格分数线(passingScore)。考试创建后默认状态为"未发布"(UNPUBLISHED)，
需要管理员手动发布后才能供学生参加。

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REQ-002 | 题目管理 | Priority: HIGH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
系统应支持题目的增删改查(CRUD)操作。题目类型包括：
  - 单选题(SINGLE_CHOICE)
  - 多选题(MULTI_CHOICE)
  - 判断题(TRUE_FALSE)
  - 简答题(ESSAY)
每道题目需包含题干内容、选项(客观题)、正确答案、难度等级和分值。

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REQ-003 | 试卷生成 | Priority: HIGH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Exam paper generation: The system shall support automatic paper generation
by randomly selecting questions from the question bank based on difficulty
level (EASY, MEDIUM, HARD). Administrators can specify the number of questions
for each type and difficulty to compose the exam paper.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REQ-004 | 学生参加考试 | Priority: HIGH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
学生可以开始考试并在线答题。系统需要记录考试开始时间，当考试时间超过
规定时长(duration)后，系统应自动提交试卷(auto-submit on timeout)。
学生在考试过程中可以保存答案，最终提交后不可修改。

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REQ-005 | 客观题自动评分 | Priority: HIGH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Auto grading for objective questions: The system shall automatically grade
single choice, multiple choice, and true/false questions by comparing
student answers with the correct answers. 多选题需要全部选对才得分。

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REQ-006 | 主观题人工阅卷 | Priority: MEDIUM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
简答题(ESSAY)类型题目需要教师手动评分。系统应提供人工阅卷工作流，
教师可以查看学生答案并给出分数和评语。All essay questions must be
manually reviewed before the final score is published.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REQ-007 | 成绩统计与排名 | Priority: MEDIUM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
系统应提供考试成绩的统计分析功能，包括：
  - 平均分(average score)
  - 最高分(highest score)
  - 最低分(lowest score)
  - 及格率(pass rate)
  - 学生排名(ranking)
统计结果应支持按考试维度查看。

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REQ-008 | 防作弊检测 | Priority: MEDIUM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Anti-cheating detection: The system shall detect potential cheating behaviors
including 切换标签页检测(switch tab detection) and 重复答案检测
(duplicate answer detection). When cheating is detected, the system should
flag the exam record for further review by the instructor.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REQ-009 | 成绩复核与申诉 | Priority: LOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
学生可以在成绩公布后申请成绩复核。Reexamination and appeal workflow:
学生提交申诉请求 -> 教师审核 -> 给出复核结果。
每个申诉需要有明确的申诉理由和处理状态。

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REQ-010 | 导出考试结果 | Priority: LOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Export exam results to report: The system shall support exporting exam
results and statistics into a readable report format. 报告应包含学生姓名、
各题得分、总分、排名等信息，支持纯文本格式导出。

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REQ-011 | 题库导入导出 | Priority: LOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Question bank import/export: The system shall support batch import and
export of questions. 支持批量操作，导入时需要进行格式校验，
导出时按题目类型和难度分类整理。
